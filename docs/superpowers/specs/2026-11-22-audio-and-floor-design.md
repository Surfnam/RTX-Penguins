# Audio system and the Ice Cave floor — design

## Intent
Add sound effects and state-aware background music to RTX Penguins, and add one new unique floor (this teammate's level, one per person).

## Goals
- Music that follows the game: menu/paused, exploring, danger (near turret or enemy), plus win and lose stingers, with crossfades.
- SFX for jump, land, dash, coin, pickup, potion, checkpoint, puzzle piece, death, menu buttons, win, lose, NPC and turret.
- A new floor with its own layout and frozen-cave look, using the existing 4-piece puzzle, hazards and exit.

## Non-goals
- Shop, camera and animation work (separate PRs).
- Reworking existing levels or menus beyond adding sound hooks.

## Anti-goals
- A missing clip must never throw or break gameplay.
- No Claude/Anthropic attribution in commits; every commit is one short sentence.

## Constraints
- Unity 6000.0.82f1, URP; no Unity editor available while authoring, so scenes are generated by an editor menu script like LevelBuilder.
- Only on what is in origin/main (camera PR #9 and shop PR #10 are not merged; Zero's arctic assets were never pushed).
- Music: Blue Lava "Melodic Winter" (CC BY-SA, credit required), downloaded by the user. SFX: Kenney packs (CC0).

## Approaches
1. Generate original audio in code: always available but simple sounding. Not chosen.
2. Free packs wired into a code-created AudioManager (chosen): better sound, small licensing note (credits).
3. Hand-edit scenes to add AudioSources everywhere: fragile YAML edits. Not chosen.

## Design (chosen)
- `AudioManager` singleton created at runtime (RuntimeInitializeOnLoad, same pattern as LevelRuntimeBootstrap). Loads clips from `Resources/Audio/{Music,SFX}` by name; missing clips are skipped.
- Music state machine with two crossfading AudioSources: Menu, Explore, Danger, plus Win/Lose stingers. Respects `AudioListener.volume` (menu slider).
- SFX hooks added with small calls (`AudioManager.PlaySfx(...)`) in PlayerController, Pickup, Checkpoint, PuzzlePiece, PenguinMenuController, MenuStateTrigger, Turret, EnemyNPC.
- `FloorIceCaveBuilder` editor menu item (`RTX Penguins > Build Ice Cave Floor`) generates `Assets/Scenes/FloorIceCave.unity`, adds it to Build Settings.
- Credits for Blue Lava and Kenney go in the README.

## Left to the builder
Exact layout and hazard placement of the Ice Cave floor, clip-to-event mapping, volume levels.
